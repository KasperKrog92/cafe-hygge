# Fleur de Lune — game direction

Lunafreya has acquired a neglected café. She wears her blonde hair in a bun;
she is a character with a voice of her own, not a silent management cursor.
Through her, the player builds a place and becomes part of a community.

Café Hygge supplies the emotional core: warm pixel art, small routines, rain,
quiet sound, familiar faces and pleasure in simply watching life unfold. Its
finished interior is neither our starting room nor our maximum scope.

## Start with a room

The first milestone is an abandoned room made into the smallest functioning
**Fleur de Lune**. Clear rubble, sweep, repair windows and surfaces, bring in
second-hand furniture and obtain enough equipment to serve the first patron.
Every action should visibly change the space. Make that transformation tactile
and satisfying before introducing a broad management system.

## Grow a place and a community

The longer direction is ruin → tiny café → neighborhood café → beloved
gathering place → something shaped by Lunafreya and the people around her.
Furniture, equipment, food and drinks, helpers, adjoining rooms, reading, art,
music and gardening are possible growth paths, not commitments to build now.
Restoration, management, exploration, relationships, dialogue and quiet observation
should support one another. Neither a pure management simulator nor a pure visual
novel captures this direction.

Repeated visits introduce people with preferences, routines, histories, problems,
friendships and ambitions. Relationships are a central pillar, not decoration for
an economy. Prefer specific, intimate exchanges over constant generic dialogue.

## Safe to linger

Meaningful conversations narrow the camera toward Lunafreya and her companion.
Demanding gameplay pauses: no urgent orders, expiring timers, interruptions or
new problems. Rain, cups and other ambient routines can continue. Time spent
with a person must never cost the player progress or punish their attention.

Active restoration is required to open the café; the old rule that all interaction
is optional no longer defines this branch. Upgrades and gentle management are
welcome. Absence penalties, decay and pressure remain incompatible with the mood.

## Current scope

relationship moments and story inspiration for future development.

[game.md](game.md) records the playable restoration and **Coffee & company**
chapter: returning Holger and Astrid, coffee and tea service, small room

are player-paced; the wider growth described above remains open.
The `idle` branch is preserved. `reference.html` retains the earlier café locally
for comparison and reuse. Future features belong on the roadmap until built.
